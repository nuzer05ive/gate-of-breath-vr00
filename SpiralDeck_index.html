What is SpiralDeck?

SpiralDeck links WAiiC0D3 scroll glyphs to fully-dynamic VR worlds.
A single QR glyph carries your scroll → our loader decodes it → the Crown engine spawns guardians, breath-audio, and geometry on the fly.

🗺️ Quick Flight Checklist

  • 1. Generate or receive a WAiiC0D3 QR.
      Use generator.html to mint one, or scan a friend’s glyph.
  • 2. Open the Loader.
  • 3. Upload / scan the QR. Loader stores its scroll into localStorage.currentScroll.
  • 4. Click “Enter VR”. You’ll tunnel to the VR scene defined by the scroll’s vrLink.
  • 5. Inside the Gate…
    • Look at a guardian ➜ they’ll greet you via Web-Speech.
    • Breathe with the soundtrack (Inhale / Hold / Exhale pattern).
    • Interact with HUD panels to add new guardians or update the scroll.
  • Tip: for full immersion, open the site in a WebXR-capable browser (Quest / Android Chrome) and press the headset icon.
